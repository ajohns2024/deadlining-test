THE RESEARCH INITIATIVE

ABOUT
DEADLINING

Making fragmented records visible.

Deadlining documents missing and murdered Black women and girls in Washington, D.C., bringing scattered public records into an accessible case database and spatial research platform.

The project examines how geography, jurisdiction, reporting, and public memory shape whose stories remain visible.

01

DOCUMENT

Preserve and organize case records for missing and murdered Black women and girls in Washington, D.C.

02

CONNECT

Bring together fragmented sources, including police reports, news coverage, and public records, into a single, accessible database.

03

EXAMINE

Study spatial patterns across the District to understand how geography, jurisdiction, reporting, and public memory shape visibility.
